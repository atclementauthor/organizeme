OrganizeMe

A personal Android app for keeping a phone, inbox and accounts tidy.

  • Screens and blocks spam calls on the phone
  • Finds junk email senders, unsubscribes and auto-trashes them
  • Detects recurring subscriptions from email receipts
  • Backs up photos to the owner's Google Drive and clears old ones from the phone once their copy is verified

This is a private app used by its developer and is not available for download.
